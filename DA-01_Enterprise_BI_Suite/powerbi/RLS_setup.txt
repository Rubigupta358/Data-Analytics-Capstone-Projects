Region manager row-level security:
1. Create role RegionManager in Power BI Desktop.
2. Apply this filter to DimTerritory:
   [Region] = USERPRINCIPALNAME()
   This expression is suitable only when Region contains the signed-in email mapping.
3. For a practical email-to-region setup, create a UserRegion table with UserEmail and Region,
   relate it to DimTerritory, and use:
   UserRegion[UserEmail] = USERPRINCIPALNAME()
4. Test the role with View As. Publish and assign users in Power BI Service.
Note: Service publishing and scheduled refresh require a Power BI Service account.
